# Bug audit round 1 — simulators and interactive exam figures

Date: 2026-11-10 · Base: `af68b0f` · Scope: `modules/**` (mech, em, physics simulators and exam-figure interactivity). Read-only audit; no product files were edited.

Served with `python3 scripts/serve-dev.py . 8705`. Playwright Chromium 156, viewports `1280×900` and `390×844`, light and dark. All 14 simulator routes loaded with 0 console errors and 0 page errors; 0 `.katex-error`; all 107 distinct figures rendered.
